## ` 01 ` &nbsp;About

<table>
<tr>
<td width="62%" valign="top">

I'm a **Senior Backend Engineer** building production systems since **2014**, specialized in
**Node.js** and **Golang** with a sharp focus on **scalability, resilience, and performance**.

My work lives in the hard parts of distributed systems &mdash; **fintech rails**, **payment processing**,
**Pix and Open Finance integrations**, **event-driven pipelines**, and **cloud-native infrastructure on AWS**.
